# CoastWatch – Product Assumptions

## 1. What product are we planning to build?

We are planning to develop **CoastWatch**, a community-powered coastal pollution monitoring and cleanup coordination platform.

It is a web-based application that allows citizens and volunteers to report coastal pollution, identify pollution hotspots, participate in beach cleanup activities, and monitor environmental progress.

The platform aims to encourage community participation and make coastal pollution information easier to access and manage.

## 2. What problem does it solve?

Coastal pollution is a growing environmental concern. Plastic waste, discarded fishing equipment, and other forms of waste can accumulate along beaches and coastal areas, negatively affecting marine ecosystems.

Information about pollution incidents is often scattered across social media posts, photographs, and individual complaints. This makes it difficult for communities to identify frequently affected locations and coordinate cleanup activities.

CoastWatch aims to address this problem by providing a centralised platform where pollution incidents can be reported, monitored, and connected with community cleanup efforts.

## 3. Who are the target users?

The primary target users are:

- **Local citizens:** People who visit beaches and want to report pollution.
- **Environmental volunteers:** Individuals interested in participating in coastal cleanup activities.
- **Environmental organisations:** Groups that organise cleanup campaigns and monitor pollution.
- **Students and educational institutions:** Students who want to participate in environmental awareness and conservation activities.
- **Community coordinators:** People who monitor reports and organise local cleanup initiatives.

## 4. What is the main use case?

A citizen visiting a beach notices plastic waste accumulating near the shoreline.

They open CoastWatch and submit a pollution report by uploading a photograph, selecting the pollution category, and providing the location and description.

The report becomes visible on the platform, allowing other users to discover the affected location.

Environmental volunteers can then explore nearby reports, participate in cleanup events, and contribute towards reducing coastal pollution.

Users can also view the platform's dashboard to understand reported pollution incidents and community cleanup activities.

## 5. What are the core features?

The application will provide the following features:

1. **Pollution Reporting:** Users can submit pollution reports with photographs, descriptions, categories, and locations.

2. **Pollution Reports Dashboard:** Displays submitted reports along with their current status, category, and location.

3. **Pollution Hotspot Map:** Displays reported pollution locations geographically to help users identify affected coastal areas.

4. **Cleanup Events:** Allows users to explore upcoming beach cleanup activities and register as volunteers.

5. **Impact Dashboard:** Displays statistics such as total pollution reports, cleanup events, volunteer participation, and recorded waste collected.

6. **Report Filtering:** Users can filter reports based on pollution category, location, and status.

7. **User Profile:** Displays basic user information and their participation in cleanup activities.

## 6. What assumptions are we making about the users?

We are making the following assumptions:

- People visiting coastal areas are willing to report visible pollution if the reporting process is simple.
- Users have access to smartphones or computers and an internet connection.
- Users are willing to upload photographs and provide location information when reporting pollution.
- Environmental volunteers are interested in discovering and participating in local cleanup activities.
- Environmental organisations may find a centralised reporting platform useful for coordinating their activities.
- Users prefer a simple interface that makes reporting and discovering pollution incidents convenient.

These assumptions will need to be validated through feedback and interaction with potential users.

## 7. What will be included in our MVP?

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include the essential features required to demonstrate the main functionality of CoastWatch.

The MVP will contain:

- A landing page introducing the CoastWatch platform.
- A dashboard displaying pollution and cleanup statistics.
- A pollution reporting form.
- A reports page displaying submitted pollution incidents.
- Report filtering based on category and status.
- A pollution hotspot map using sample location data.
- A cleanup events page.
- A volunteer registration or participation feature.
- An impact dashboard.
- Reusable React components.
- React Context API for managing application-level state.
- Realistic dummy data to demonstrate application functionality.

The MVP will be developed using Next.js, React, JavaScript, and CSS.

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

To keep the initial development manageable, the following features will not be included:

- Real-time integration with government pollution monitoring systems.
- Direct communication with environmental authorities.
- AI-based pollution detection from photographs.
- IoT-based ocean pollution monitoring.
- Real payment or donation processing.
- Advanced authentication and identity verification.
- Real-time GPS tracking of volunteers.
- Automated waste collection services.
- Advanced machine learning-based pollution prediction.

These features may be considered for future versions of CoastWatch after the initial MVP has been developed and tested.
